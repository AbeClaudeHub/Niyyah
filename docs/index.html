The story

From Unayzah to Morris Park

One shop in 2009 became more than four hundred across seven countries. In 2024 it crossed an ocean — the first Saudi coffee chain ever to open in the States.

2009 · Unayzah

One small shop opens in Al-Qassim, in the Saudi interior, serving coffee and tea to a neighbourhood that keeps coming back.

Across the Kingdom

The shop becomes a chain and grows into one of the largest privately held coffee and tea retailers in Saudi Arabia.

The Gulf & beyond

Franchising carries the brand into Kuwait, Qatar, Oman, Jordan, the UAE and Iraq — sixth largest branded coffee chain in the Middle East.

2024 · The Bronx

Kyan opens at 689 Morris Park Avenue, on a stretch of the Bronx the neighbourhood calls Little Yemen.

The one they come back for

The Spanish Latte

Espresso poured over sweetened condensed milk, then cold milk and ice — served long, so the layers hold for about three seconds and then don't.

The room

Built for the second hour

Most coffee shops are designed for the first fifteen minutes. This one is designed for what happens after.

Open until midnight

Seven days a week, from seven in the morning. Late is not an exception here — it's the point. Expect a wait on weekend afternoons after two.

Halal, all of it

Every item on the board, front to back, with no asterisks and nothing you need to ask twice about.

Little Yemen

Morris Park Avenue already had the grocers, the bakeries and the late tables. Kyan joined a street that was already awake.
