# The Trader and the Nafs — Launch Page Strategy

*Prepared as a category-launch document, not a website tweak list.*
The deliverable this document defends is the rebuilt `docs/index.html`.

---

## The one decision that frames everything

The current site is a genuine 8.5/10: premium emerald-and-gold design, a 3D
spinning cover, a sharp hook ("the knowing was never the problem — the *doing*
was"). **We do not throw that away.** The elite move is not vanity-rebuilding a
beautiful thing; it is fixing the three places where the 8.5 leaks money and
adding the one mechanism that makes it category-defining.

We keep the design DNA. We rebuild the **funnel, the positioning, and the
incentive.**

## PHASE 3 + 4 — Category & positioning (the core bet)

**Category name:** *Islamic Trading Psychology.*

**Category statement:**
> There are a thousand books on **how to trade.** There is not one on **who is
> doing the trading.** Trading psychology gave that person tactics. Islam gave
> the world a complete, 1,400-year-old science of the self — the *nafs* — long
> before the first chart. *The Trader and the Nafs* is the book that finally
> puts them in the same room.

**Why the category deserves to exist / why now:** A generation of Muslim
traders has arrived at the screen with two things that have never been
addressed together — a real strategy and a real *deen*. Mainstream trading
psychology stops at "control your emotions" and has no account of *why* a person
overrides his own plan. Islamic self-development has a complete map of the lower
self but never spoke to the P&L. The seam between them is empty. This book owns
the seam.

**Why it is different from the adjacent shelves:**
- **vs. trading psychology** — those books treat you as a bundle of cognitive
  biases to debug. This treats you as a soul with a lower self to govern.
- **vs. self-help** — no life-hacks; a tradition with 1,400 years of evidence.
- **vs. Islamic self-development** — it is written *for the screen*, in the
  trader's own language of stops, sizing, and revenge trades.

**One-sentence positioning:**
> *The Trader and the Nafs* is the definitive book on Islamic trading
> psychology — for the trader who knows exactly what to do and keeps doing the
> opposite.

**Unique mechanism:** the *nafs* framework — the three selves (commanding,
reproaching, tranquil), the five diseases (impatience, greed, fear, pride,
despair), and the NAFS loop you run in the heat of the trade.

**Core transformation:** from the self that *sabotages* to the self that
*follows through* — where profitability becomes a byproduct of a governed soul.

## PHASE 5 — Waitlist: the winning incentive

**The winner: "The Nafs Diagnostic" — a 60-second assessment that reveals which
of the five diseases is silently costing you the most money** (plus your
dominant nafs-state), with the full report email-gated.

**Why it beats every static lead magnet (incl. the current Cards PDF):**
1. **Self-relevance is irresistible.** People will not download a PDF about a
   topic; they cannot resist a verdict about *themselves*.
2. **It opens a loop.** Answering six questions creates a need to see the
   result. Email is the natural unlock — a fair trade, not a toll.
3. **It auto-segments the list.** Every subscriber arrives tagged by their
   dominant disease, so the nurture sequence writes itself (the emails plan
   already maps one email per disease).
4. **It seeds the book's framework** so the book becomes the obvious full
   treatment — the diagnostic *is* a free sample of the unique mechanism.
5. **It is the category, made interactive.** No trading-psychology book has an
   Islamic *nafs* diagnostic. It is unforgeable proof the category is real.

**Runner-up concepts** (kept warm for A/B tests and ads): *Trading Discipline
Score, Self-Sabotage Audit, Trader Archetype (Khalid vs. Omar), "Which broken
rule is yours?", the Three-Selves test.* The diagnostic generalizes all of them.

**The opt-in journey:** Visit → take the Diagnostic → see partial verdict →
email to unlock full report **+ founding-reader access** → welcome email
delivers The Cards + the chapter written for *your* disease → weekly
disease-tagged nurture → launch: founding-reader first access + 72h bonus →
buy (direct or Amazon) → review request.

## PHASE 7 — Trust architecture (built for a stranger)

- **Credibility band of real numbers:** 70,000 words · 38 chapters · 6 parts ·
  every claim grounded in authentic Qur'an and hadith.
- **Founder, for cold traffic:** Abrihym Aobad, founder of **Niyyah** — the
  community of **700+** traders learning to trade without losing themselves. The
  book was written from a real failure (the Tuesday-stop story), not a podcast.
- **Authentic proof, not fabricated reviews:** lead with community scale + a
  pull-quote from the manuscript itself (sells the writing honestly); reserve a
  clearly-marked slot for the 30+ permissioned advance reviews the ARC program
  collects at launch. **No invented named testimonials.**

## PHASE 8 — Urgency (authentic, no fake timers)

**The Founding-Reader framework.** The waitlist is not a notification list; it
is the founding cohort.
- First access the day it launches — before Amazon, before anyone.
- The **Founding-Reader bonus**: the 12-Week Workbook + printable Cards, free
  with any edition — reserved for the waitlist, the first **72 hours** only.
- A seat in the launch cohort inside the Niyyah community.
- The pre-launch nurture (the ideas behind the cards) only the early get.

No countdown clocks. The scarcity is real: the bonus window and the
founding-cohort framing are true and defensible.

## Wiring note (the only thing the owner must finish)

In `docs/index.html`, set `WAITLIST_ENDPOINT` (top of the script) to your
MailerLite/Kit form-action URL. Until then the page runs in honest demo mode:
it shows success and delivers the reward, and logs captured leads to the
console + `localStorage` so nothing is lost during testing. Diagnostic result
is submitted alongside the email as a `disease` field for segmentation.
