The four saboteurs

Four voices. One steering wheel.

كِبْر

Kibr · Ego

Arrogance. The refusal to be wrong.

“The market is overreacting. It needs room. One more candle.”

طَمَع

Tamaʿ · Greed

The appetite that never says enough.

“You're seeing it well today. One more clean trade. Don't waste the rhythm.”

غَضَب

Ghadab · Anger

Trading as retaliation.

“It hunted your stop. You're not ending the day red. Get it back. Now.”

وَسْوَاس

Waswās · Doubt

The whisper. Hesitation, second-guessing, the chase.

“Wait for confirmation. Check what the others think. Take the profit before it leaves.”

The audit's twenty questions map to these four. You won't see which question scores which voice. You'll feel it.
