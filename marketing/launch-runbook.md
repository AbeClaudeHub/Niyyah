# Launch-Day Runbook — *The Trader and the Nafs*

Concentrate sales into **48–72 hours** to drive rank and momentum. Everything is pre-written
and scheduled by the day before. Default launch offer: **bonus over discount** — the
printable Cards + 12-Week Workbook free with any edition, first 72 hours.

---

## T-minus 1 day (the night before)
- [ ] Direct store (Gumroad/Stripe) product live & tested — buy a test copy, confirm delivery.
- [ ] Amazon: Kindle pre-order converts at launch; paperback proof approved & live.
- [ ] Landing page flipped from **waitlist** → **buy** (CTAs point to [STORE]/[AMAZON]).
- [ ] All launch emails scheduled (L-1 already sent; L0 / L0b / L+1 / L+2 queued).
- [ ] All social posts drafted + scheduled; pinned posts ready.
- [ ] ARC readers reminded: post honest reviews tomorrow. Review link tested.
- [ ] Bonus delivery mechanism tested (how buyers receive Workbook + Cards).

## Launch day (L0) — hour by hour
- [ ] **08:00** — Email **L0** to full list. Flip landing page live.
- [ ] **08:15** — Discord **@everyone** announcement + pin. Drop buy links.
- [ ] **08:30** — IG/TikTok launch post + Stories (link sticker). Update all bios → buy link.
- [ ] **Midday** — ARC reviews going live; screenshot the first few → Stories/Discord.
- [ ] **17:00** — **L0b** resend to non-openers (new subject). Story reminder of 72h bonus.
- [ ] **Evening (optional)** — Discord/IG **Live**: read the Ch.1 opening, Q&A, thank readers.
- [ ] Reply to every comment/DM. Momentum is social.

## L+1
- [ ] Email **L+1** with real early-reader quotes. Fresh Reel/TikTok ("launch is live").
- [ ] Repost reviews/reactions. Keep bonus visible.

## L+2 (bonus closes)
- [ ] Email **L+2** "bonus closes tonight." Final Stories countdown. Then stop pushing.

## L+5 → ongoing
- [ ] Email buyers segment: **review request**.
- [ ] Add best real testimonials to the landing page testimonials slot.
- [ ] Switch funnel to **evergreen** (lead magnet → nurture → buy).
- [ ] Review numbers: if conversion is solid, test **Amazon Ads** + **Meta retargeting** of
      landing-page visitors. Upsell the Complete Kit.

---

## Channel cheat-sheet (where each sale comes from)
- **Warm audience (Discord/email/social)** → send to **direct store** first (your margin, you
  keep the email). They'll buy regardless of platform.
- **Cold/searching buyers** → **Amazon** (discovery + credibility + reviews + New Release badge).
- Concentrate timing so both spike together on L0.

## Metrics to watch
- Launch 48–72h units (aim: 70%+ of month-1 sales here).
- Amazon Best Seller Rank / "#1 New Release" category badge.
- Reviews live in week 1 (aim 15–30, ARC-driven).
- Email → sale conversion (your best channel; learn from it for the evergreen funnel).
