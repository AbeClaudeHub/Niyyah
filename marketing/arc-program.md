# Advance Reader (ARC) Program — *The Trader and the Nafs*

**Why it matters:** launch-week reviews are the single biggest lever on Amazon. A book that
goes live with 15–30 honest reviews ranks and converts far better than one with zero. Your
700+ Discord is the perfect recruiting ground. **Goal: 20–50 committed advance readers.**

**Rules (keep it clean / TOS-safe):**
- Reviews must be **honest and the reader's own words.** Never trade a copy *for* a positive
  review — you give a free copy in exchange for an **honest review**. (Amazon prohibits
  incentivised positive reviews.)
- Amazon reviews ideally come from accounts with some purchase history; suggest reviewers also
  grab the low-priced Kindle on launch day so the review is "Verified Purchase."
- Collect reviews ready to **post on launch day**, not before the listing is live.

---

## 1) Discord recruitment post (pin in #founding-readers)

> **📖 Want an advance copy of *The Trader and the Nafs*?**
>
> I'm looking for **30 readers** to read it before anyone else and leave an **honest review**
> on launch day — good, bad, or brutal. That's the whole deal: a free advance copy, your
> honest words.
>
> You're a fit if you actually trade (or have), you'll read it in ~2 weeks, and you'll post a
> short honest review the day it launches.
>
> React 📖 and I'll DM you the copy + a simple guide. First 30. 🤲

---

## 2) Reader instructions (DM / PDF to each ARC reader)

> **Thank you.** Three things:
> 1. **Read it within 2 weeks** (by [DATE]). Mark anything that hits you — I may ask to quote
>    a few (with your permission).
> 2. **On launch day ([DATE])** post an honest review — Amazon (link coming) and/or Goodreads.
>    3–5 sentences is plenty. Your real reaction > a perfect write-up.
> 3. **Be honest.** If something didn't land, say so. Honest reviews help the right readers
>    find it and keep the whole thing credible.
>
> What helps a review: who you are (a trader who…), what problem it spoke to, the one idea you
> took away, and who you'd hand it to.
