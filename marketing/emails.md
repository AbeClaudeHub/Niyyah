# Email Sequences — *The Trader and the Nafs*

Tool: **Kit** or **MailerLite** (free tier). Replace `[DATE]`, `[STORE_LINK]`,
`[AMAZON_LINK]`, `[CARDS_LINK]`. Tone = the book's voice: honest, direct, a little
uncomfortable, never hypey. All quotes below are from the manuscript.

---

## A) Welcome / lead-magnet delivery (fires on signup)

**Subject:** Here are The Cards
**Body:**
> You're in.
>
> Attached (and here: [CARDS_LINK]) is **The Cards** — the whole book compressed to what
> you can actually glance at in the heat, when there's no time to remember a chapter. Print
> them. Tape them where you trade.
>
> Start with one line: *The knowing was never the problem. The doing was.*
>
> Over the next few weeks I'll send you the ideas behind the cards — the five diseases, the
> one loop that beats them, and the day the market pulled a stranger out of my chest.
>
> *The Trader and the Nafs* is out **[DATE]**. Waitlist gets first access and the launch bonus.
>
> — Abrihym

---

## B) Pre-launch nurture (1 email/week, value-first)

**Email 1 — The mirror.** *Subject:* The market didn't create him. It revealed him.
> Tell the Tuesday-stop story in 150 words → "the market is one of the most honest mirrors a
> human can stand in front of." Soft P.S.: out [DATE], join us early.

**Email 2 — The real enemy.** *Subject:* The one name that never makes your enemies list.
> Market makers, the platform, the "they" — the list is long, and none of it can move your
> stop. There's one enemy you actually have authority over. P.S. pre-order is open: [STORE_LINK].

**Email 3 — The five diseases.** *Subject:* Which of the five is yours?
> One-line each (impatience/greed/fear/pride/despair) + the cure. "Name yours — the naming is
> half the cure." CTA: pre-order.

**Email 4 — The loop.** *Subject:* One enemy. One loop.
> N → A → F → S, run it the instant the heat rises. CTA: pre-order + "launch in [X] days."

**Email 5 — Launch offer reveal.** *Subject:* What you get if you're early.
> Announce the 72h launch bonus (Workbook + printable Cards with any edition). Date + time.

---

## C) Launch week (the money sequence)

**L-1 — 24 hours.** *Subject:* Tomorrow.
> "Tomorrow the book is live. The launch bonus runs 72 hours. Be ready." One link.

**L0 — Launch day (morning).** *Subject:* It's live: The Trader and the Nafs
> Short. The promise ("stop chasing how to become profitable; become the person profit
> follows"), the formats, the bonus, two buttons: **Get it direct** [STORE_LINK] /
> **On Amazon** [AMAZON_LINK]. Ask for a review if it helps them.

**L0b — Launch day (evening, openers-only resend).** *Subject:* Don't lose the bonus
> Resend to non-openers with a new subject. Same offer, urgency on the 72h window.

**L+1 — Momentum + social proof.** *Subject:* "Who was that?" — early readers are feeling seen
> 2–3 real early-reader quotes (with permission). Bonus still on. Buttons.

**L+2 — Last call.** *Subject:* Bonus closes tonight
> Final 12h of the launch bonus. One idea, one link. Then stop.

**L+5 — Review request (to buyers segment).** *Subject:* A small ask
> "If it moved something, a one-line review on Amazon helps more than you'd think." Direct link.

---

## D) Evergreen (post-launch, ongoing)
- New subscribers keep getting sequence **A → B**, ending in a standing CTA to buy
  ([STORE_LINK]/[AMAZON_LINK]) — the funnel runs itself.
- Monthly: one genuinely useful email (a disease, a card, a reflection) → soft CTA.

### Deliverability notes
- Warm up: send to engaged Discord folks first.
- One clear CTA per email. Plain-text style outperforms heavy design for this audience.
- Segment buyers vs non-buyers after launch so you stop pitching people who already bought.
