## PHASE 1 — BRUTAL AUDIT (current site, ranked by business impact)

The current page is genuinely an 8.5/10 on **craft** (design, motion, typography, voice). It is a
**5/10 on conversion architecture**. The gap between those two numbers is the entire opportunity.

### 🔴 Critical leaks (fix these or nothing else matters)

**1. The waitlist has no reason to exist. (Highest-impact issue on the site.)**
The two "free reads" — Chapter 1 and **The Cards** — are downloadable *without an email* from the nav,
the free-reads grid, **and** the form handler itself (`window.open('…the-cards.pdf')` fires on submit
regardless of email). The single thing we ask people to trade their email for is **already free, three
ways, above the fold**. We have built a beautiful machine for giving away the asset and capturing
nothing. Every visitor who wanted the magnet got it without converting. *Expected impact of fixing:
2–4× opt-in rate.*

**2. There is no category.** The page is positioned as "the book about the soul behind the screen" —
beautiful, but a *mood*, not a *category*. Category-defining books (Atomic Habits → "habit formation,"
Psychology of Money → "behavioral finance for normal people") **name and own a territory**. This book
has an empty, uncontested, perfectly-sized territory available to it — *Islamic Trading Psychology* —
and currently plants no flag in it. *Impact: this is the difference between "interesting book" and "the
book Muslim traders have been waiting for."*

**3. The opt-in is generic.** "Enter your email → Join the Waitlist" is a 2010 mechanic. The book is
*literally built around a diagnostic structure* — the **Five Diseases** and the **Three Selves**. We are
sitting on the highest-converting lead magnet in the entire genre (a personalized assessment) and using
a plain email box instead. *Impact: a diagnostic typically converts 25–55% of engaged traffic vs.
3–8% for a bare email field.*

**4. The form doesn't capture anything.** It's a demo handler (`return false`). No ESP, no storage, no
confirmation. Even motivated users currently cannot actually join. *Impact: 100% of intent is lost.*

### 🟠 High-impact gaps

**5. Cold-traffic trust is thin.** Assume the visitor has never heard of Abrihym. The story is strong,
but there is no credibility scaffolding: the **700+ member Niyyah community** (named in your own
marketing docs!) appears nowhere. No "why trust this man on *both* trading *and* deen" bridge.

**6. Urgency is a whisper.** "The waitlist gets first access" is not urgency; it's a fact. There is no
*founding-reader* identity, no finite benefit, no reason today beats next month.

**7. The strongest copy in the book is buried.** "*Today is different* — the password the lower self
uses to get past the guard." "*Who was that?*" "The failure happened in the six inches between my brain
and my hand." These are the most visceral lines you have, and they're in the manuscript, not on the page.

**8. Placeholder testimonials are live.** Three "Anonymous / Discord member" quotes with a `TODO` to
replace them. Cold traffic smells these instantly; fake-feeling proof is worse than none.

### 🟡 Polish

**9. Single CTA destination.** Every button does the same thing (scroll to one email box). No laddering
of commitment.
**10. No meta-narrative of size.** Nothing makes the visitor feel "this is bigger than a book" — no
manifesto, no movement, no "one card" creed moment (which exists in the manuscript and is *perfect* for this).
