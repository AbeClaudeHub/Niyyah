# The Trader and the Nafs

**Why You Keep Breaking Your Rules — and How Islam Teaches You to Master Yourself**

*by Abrihym Aobad, Founder of Niyyah*

---

The complete manuscript for a book on Islamic psychology and trading self-mastery.

This is not a trading-strategy book. It is a book about self-mastery — about the gap between
what a trader *knows* and what he *does*, and how the 1,400-year-old Islamic science of the
*nafs* (the self) teaches a person to close it. The market is treated throughout as a mirror
of the soul: every broken rule a confession, every loss a piece of information about the self,
every session an opportunity for *tazkiyah* (purification).

## Manuscript

The full manuscript lives in [`manuscript/`](manuscript/), one file per chapter, numbered in
reading order. Start with [`TABLE-OF-CONTENTS.md`](manuscript/TABLE-OF-CONTENTS.md).

- **38 chapters** across **6 parts**, plus front matter and two appendices
- **~70,000 words**
- Two recurring traders followed across the book — Khalid, the brilliant everytrader who keeps sabotaging himself, and Omar, the quiet master
- Each chapter includes: an opening story, a psychological insight, an Islamic insight
  (grounded in authentic Qur'an and hadith), a practical trading application, reflection
  questions, and a training exercise.
- **Appendix A — The Twelve-Week Path:** the book's exercises sequenced into a single
  do-this-in-order program.
- **Appendix B — The Cards:** quick-reference summaries (the NAFS loop, the three selves, the
  Catalogue of lies, the five diseases, the seven strengths, the pre-market and daily-reckoning
  checklists).

## Structure

- **Part I — The Invisible War:** seeing the real enemy clearly
- **Part II — Understanding the Nafs:** the self and its three states
- **Part III — The Five Diseases:** impatience, greed, fear, pride, despair
- **Part IV — The Prophetic Psychology of Performance:** sabr, tawakkul, taqwa, muhasabah, ikhlas, ihsan, shukr
- **Part V — Building the Muslim Trader:** identity, rules, and the daily operating system
- **Part VI — The Path to Mastery:** why profitability is a byproduct of self-mastery
